# Physical AI Overview

## What is Physical AI?

Physical AI represents the convergence of artificial intelligence with the physical world through embodied systems. Unlike traditional AI that operates purely in digital spaces, Physical AI involves intelligent systems that perceive, reason, and act in real-world environments.

## Key Concepts

Physical AI systems combine:
- **Perception**: Sensing and interpreting physical environments
- **Cognition**: Processing information to make decisions
- **Action**: Executing physical behaviors to achieve goals
- **Embodiment**: Having a physical form that interacts with the world

## The Role of Humanoid Robots

Humanoid robots serve as particularly compelling platforms for Physical AI research and deployment because:

1. Their form factor is naturally compatible with human environments
2. They can perform tasks designed for human capabilities
3. They provide insights into human intelligence through embodied cognition
4. They enable natural human-robot interaction

## Challenges in Physical AI

Working with Physical AI systems introduces unique challenges:

- Real-time processing requirements for dynamic environments
- Safety considerations for systems operating near humans
- The reality gap between simulation and real-world performance
- Complex integration of multiple sensing and actuation modalities
- Uncertainty management in unstructured environments

These challenges make robust system architecture and reliable communication protocols fundamental to Physical AI success.
